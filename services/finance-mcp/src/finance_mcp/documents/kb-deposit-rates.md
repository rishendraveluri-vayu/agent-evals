## How fixed deposit (FD) interest is paid

On a cumulative fixed deposit, interest compounds quarterly and is paid with the principal at maturity. A senior citizen is a depositor aged 60 or over on the date of the deposit. The rate is fixed on the date of the deposit for its whole tenure.
