## Do senior citizens get a higher fixed deposit rate?

Yes. A depositor aged 60 or over gets 0.50 percentage point above the general rate on every fixed and recurring deposit tenure. The rate card lists both rates.

## What is the Kaveri 444 deposit?

A fixed deposit for exactly 444 days, from ₹10,000, at the rate on the rate card for "Kaveri 444". It cannot be renewed for 444 days again at the same rate.

## Can I prepay my home loan without a charge?

Yes, if it is on a floating rate: Kaveri Bank charges nothing to prepay or close a floating-rate home loan. Personal, two-wheeler and car loans have their own rules (see the prepayment and foreclosure rules).

## How is my personal loan rate decided?

By your CIBIL score when the loan is approved, from the loan products sheet. The rate is then fixed for the whole tenure.

## Where do the RBI and income tax figures come from?

From the monthly rates and rules bulletin, which cites the official source for each figure. The bank's own rates, rules and charges are in its rate card, loan sheet, prepayment rules and schedule of charges.
