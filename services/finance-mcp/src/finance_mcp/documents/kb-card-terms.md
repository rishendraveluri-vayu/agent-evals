## Interest on credit card balances

If the total amount due is not paid in full by the due date, interest is charged at 3.50% a month (42.00% a year) on the unpaid balance and on new purchases, from the date of each transaction, until the balance is cleared.

## Minimum amount due

The minimum amount due is 5% of the total amount due, or ₹200, whichever is higher, plus any EMI instalments on the card. Paying at least the minimum amount due by the due date avoids the late payment fee, but interest is still charged on the unpaid balance.

## Interest-free period

Up to 50 days on purchases, from the purchase date to the due date, when the previous statement was paid in full.

## Paying late

When the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, a late payment fee is charged (see the schedule of charges). Kaveri Bank reports a payment more than 30 days past due to the credit bureaus.
