## Personal loans for salaried customers

Kaveri Bank's personal loans have a fixed rate for the whole tenure, set by the CIBIL score at approval:

| CIBIL score | Rate (percent a year) |
| --- | --- |
| 780 and above | 11.85% |
| 740 to 779 | 12.60% |
| 700 to 739 | 14.25% |
| Below 700 | Not offered |

Loan amount ₹50,000 to ₹25 lakh; tenure 12 to 60 months. For the processing fee see the schedule of charges; for prepayment see the prepayment and foreclosure rules.

## Two-wheeler loans

A fixed rate of 11.40% a year for a CIBIL score of 740 and above, and 12.90% a year below 740. Up to 95% of the on-road price, for 12 to 48 months.

## Car loans

New cars: 9.35% a year for a CIBIL score of 750 and above, 10.10% a year below 750. Used cars up to 5 years old: 12.75% a year. Up to 7 years.

## Home loans: a floating rate linked to the repo rate

Kaveri Bank's home loans have a floating rate linked to the RBI policy repo rate, through its External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR).

- EBLR = the RBI policy repo rate + 2.65 percentage points.
- Home loan rate = EBLR + a spread set by the CIBIL score at approval: 750 and above, no spread (+0.00); 700 to 749, +0.45 percentage point; below 700, +1.05 percentage points.
- The rate resets on the first day of the month after a change in the repo rate. The spread stays the same for the life of the loan.

Up to 30 years. For the current repo rate see the rates and rules bulletin.
