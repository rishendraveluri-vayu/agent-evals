## About this bulletin

Kaveri Bank's monthly summary, for its advisers, of the official RBI and income tax figures they are asked about most, compiled on 6 November 2026. Each figure names the official source it was checked against on that date. Anything not listed here was not checked for this edition.

## RBI policy rates

Set by the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee at its meeting of 5 to 7 October 2026 (announced 7 October 2026), and still in force on 6 November 2026:

- Policy repo rate: 5.50%, raised from 5.25%.
- Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate: 5.25%.
- Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate and Bank Rate: 5.75%.

The next meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee is scheduled for 2 to 4 December 2026.

Source: Reserve Bank of India, "Resolution of the Monetary Policy Committee, October 5 to 7, 2026", press release of 7 October 2026, https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63742 (checked 6 November 2026).

## Income tax: the Income-tax Act, 2025 and the tax year

Income for the year from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 is taxed under the Income-tax Act, 2025, which uses a "tax year" in place of the old "previous year" and "assessment year". The current tax year is 2026-27.

Sources: Ministry of Finance, "Memorandum Explaining the Provisions in the Finance Bill, 2026", Part II, "Rates of income-tax in respect of income liable to tax for the tax year 2026-27 for the purposes of Income-tax Act, 2025", https://indiabudget.gov.in/doc/memo.pdf; Income Tax Department, "Objective and scope of the New Act", https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/objective-and-scope-new-act (both checked 6 November 2026).

## Income tax slabs for individuals, new (default) regime, tax year 2026-27

The rates in section 202 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, on total income. Each rate applies only to the part of the income that falls in its band.

| Total income | Rate of tax |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹4,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 | 20% |
| ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 | 25% |
| Above ₹24,00,000 | 30% |

These are the default rates; a taxpayer may opt out of them under section 202(4). The Finance Bill, 2026 proposed no change to them.

Source: Ministry of Finance, "Memorandum Explaining the Provisions in the Finance Bill, 2026", Part II, "Tax rates under section 202", https://indiabudget.gov.in/doc/memo.pdf (checked 6 November 2026).

## Not covered by this edition
