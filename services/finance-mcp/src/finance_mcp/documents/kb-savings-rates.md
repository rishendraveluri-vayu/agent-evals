## Savings account interest rates, effective 1 October 2026

Interest is worked out on the daily balance and credited every quarter. Each slab's rate applies to the part of the balance in that slab.

| Daily balance | Rate (percent a year) |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹1 lakh | 2.70% |
| Above ₹1 lakh up to ₹10 lakh | 3.05% |
| Above ₹10 lakh | 3.40% |

## Minimum balance

A monthly average balance of ₹5,000 in metro and urban branches, and ₹2,000 in semi-urban and rural branches. The charge for a shortfall is ₹350 a month, before GST.
