## Home loans on a floating rate

Kaveri Bank charges nothing to prepay part of a floating-rate home loan, or to close it in full, at any time and from any source of funds.

## Personal loans: part-prepayment and foreclosure

Part-prepayment is allowed once 6 EMIs have been paid: up to 25% of the principal outstanding, once in any 12 months, free of charge.

Foreclosure (closing the loan in full) is allowed once 6 EMIs have been paid. The foreclosure charge is a share of the principal outstanding on the day of closure:

| EMIs paid | Foreclosure charge |
| --- | --- |
| 6 to 12 | 4.50% of the principal outstanding |
| 13 to 24 | 3.25% of the principal outstanding |
| 25 or more | 2.00% of the principal outstanding |

## Two-wheeler loans: foreclosure

A two-wheeler loan can be closed in full once 6 EMIs have been paid; part-prepayment is not offered. The foreclosure charge is 4.75% of the principal outstanding if 6 to 12 EMIs have been paid, and 2.75% of the principal outstanding if 13 or more EMIs have been paid.

## Car loans: foreclosure

A car loan can be closed in full at any time. The charge is 5.25% of the principal outstanding within the first 12 EMIs, 3.25% from the 13th to the 24th EMI, and nothing after 24 EMIs.

## Taxes on these charges

Prepayment and foreclosure charges are shown before GST, which is added at the rate in force.
