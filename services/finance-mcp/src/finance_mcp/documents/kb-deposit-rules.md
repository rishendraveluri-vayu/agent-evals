## Closing a fixed deposit (FD) early: premature withdrawal

A Kaveri Bank fixed deposit can be closed before it matures. Interest is then paid at the rate on the bank's rate card, on the date of the deposit, for the period the deposit actually ran, less a penalty of 1.00 percentage point (0.50 percentage point for senior citizens). Interest already credited at a higher rate is recovered from the payout. A deposit closed within 7 days earns no interest.

Example: a deposit opened for 3 years and closed after 1 year earns the rate for "1 year to less than 2 years" that applied when it was opened, less the penalty.

## Loan or overdraft against a fixed deposit

You can borrow up to 90% of the deposit's principal against it, at 1.50 percentage points above the deposit's own rate. The deposit keeps earning its interest.

## Renewal at maturity

A deposit that is not claimed at maturity is renewed for the same tenure at the rate on the rate card on the renewal date.
