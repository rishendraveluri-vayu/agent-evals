## Loan processing fees

Charged once, when the loan is disbursed, before GST:

- Personal loan: 1.75% of the loan amount, at least ₹2,999 and at most ₹17,500.
- Two-wheeler loan: 2.25% of the loan amount, at least ₹1,499.
- Car loan: a flat ₹6,500.
- Home loan: 0.40% of the loan amount, at most ₹15,000.

## Payment and EMI charges

- EMI or NACH mandate bounce: ₹649 for each bounced instalment.
- Cheque return: ₹649 for each cheque.
- Duplicate repayment schedule or statement: ₹199.

## Credit card charges

A late payment fee is charged when the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date. It depends on the total amount due on the statement:

| Total amount due | Late payment fee |
| --- | --- |
| Up to ₹500 | Nil |
| ₹501 to ₹5,000 | ₹525 |
| ₹5,001 to ₹10,000 | ₹775 |
| ₹10,001 to ₹25,000 | ₹975 |
| ₹25,001 to ₹50,000 | ₹1,150 |
| Above ₹50,000 | ₹1,375 |

Cash withdrawal on a credit card: 2.50% of the amount, at least ₹500. Foreign currency transactions: a mark-up of 3.25%.

## Taxes on these charges

Every charge on this schedule is shown before GST, which is added at the rate in force.
